# Pixel Run

A browser platform game with a hand-built, 16-bit-inspired take on the classic World 1-1 course layout. The level follows its familiar sequence of opening blocks, pipe run, hidden reward, coin rows, brick formations, stairways, and flagpole. Artwork is drawn in code; no game image or sprite files are bundled.

## Play

Play online at <https://daveisinthehouse.github.io/mario/>.

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Use **← / →** or **A / D** to move, and **Space**, **↑**, or **W** to jump. Collect coins, hit blocks from below, grow with mushrooms, stomp enemies, and reach the flag. The **SOUND** button toggles the synthesized ground-theme music and effects.

No build tools or dependencies are required. Your high score is saved in the browser.

## Map reference

The course layout is based on the publicly viewable [World 1-1 map](https://www.mariowiki.com/File:SMB_NES_World_1-1_Map.png) and its [level overview](https://www.mariowiki.com/World_1-1_(Super_Mario_Bros.)).
